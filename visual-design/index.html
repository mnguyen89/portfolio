Visual Design

Six years of brand identity, illustration and print — including the campaign art for SoundHound’s 200-million-user milestone. Built as systems, not one-offs, so a small team could extend the work without me in the room.

Illustration & Iconography

Icon libraries and illustration sets for product, support and marketing surfaces. I fixed the grid, stroke weight and corner radius up front, then tested every glyph filled against stroked at small sizes — so the set stayed legible as it grew, and any designer could draw the next icon on spec.

Branding

Identity and campaign work, including the milestone artwork announcing SoundHound’s 200 millionth user. Every piece had to carry one idea and hold up at every size it shipped at — app icon to launch banner to holiday card.

Print

Self-directed posters and typeface specimens, where type does the work of an image. Print gives you no hover state and no second screen — which is where I learned the hierarchy and restraint I still bring to product UI.
